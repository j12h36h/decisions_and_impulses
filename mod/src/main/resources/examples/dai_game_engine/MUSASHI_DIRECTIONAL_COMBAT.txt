DAI Musashi directional combat integration
===========================================

Client bridge:
  io.github.j12h36h.dai.client.combat.DAI_MusashiDirectionalCombat

Controls while holding musashi_story:katana / nodachi / wakizashi / naginata:
  Hold LMB  = draw blade and capture camera-origin.
  Move view = choose one of 8 screen-space slash directions.
  Release   = execute chosen slash.
  Release without >=5 degrees of directional motion = forward stab.
  Hold RMB  = half-draw diagonal guard.

Vanilla startAttack / continueAttack / startUseItem are suppressed only while
one of those Musashi blades is in the main hand. The physical key mappings are
not suppressed, allowing the companion resource pack's keybind_down item-model
conditions to continue rendering draw and guard poses.
